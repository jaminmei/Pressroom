# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and security.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
